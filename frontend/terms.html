This is a free, informational demo of a warehouse order-batching and picker-routing project, operated by the project author. By using it you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

2. Demo and sample data only

All figures, batches, routes, times and percentages shown come from a bundled sample dataset in a single precomputed run. They do not describe any real warehouse and are not measured operational results. Estimated times rely on assumed values (walking speed 1.2 m/s and 18 seconds per stop) that may not match any real site.

3. Results are not guaranteed to be optimal

  • Batching uses Google OR-Tools CP-SAT with a fixed time limit. It may return a feasible solution that is not proven optimal.
  • Routing uses greedy, nearest-neighbour and 2-opt methods. These are heuristics and do not guarantee the shortest possible route.
  • Improvements seen on the sample data (for example, distance reductions versus the baseline) are specific to that data and run. They are not a promise or forecast of savings in any other warehouse.
